# 2 BHK & 3 BHK Flats in Hadapsar Annexe vs Kharadi: The 2026 Comparison

When evaluating residential real estate in Pune East, prospective homeowners and tech professionals frequently compare **Hadapsar Annexe (Manjari)** with **Kharadi**. Here is an objective comparison of pricing, carpet efficiency, social infrastructure, and connectivity.

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

---

## 2. Infrastructure & Transit Corridors
- **Kharadi Core**: Direct proximity to EON IT Park Phase 1 & 2 and World Trade Center, with peak-hour congestion on Nagar Road.
- **Kumar Magnacity (Hadapsar Annexe)**: Strategic midpoint between Magarpatta Cybercity (10 mins) and Kharadi via the upcoming river bridges, offering access to the proposed Ring Road.

---

## 3. Integrated Amenities: Standalone vs 150-Acre Township
While standalone developments in Kharadi offer standard amenities, Kumar Magnacity provides an integrated **~1 Lakh sq.ft clubhouse**, an on-campus **Podar International School**, Olympic sports courts, and 25+ acres of peripheral green parks.

---

## 4. Rental Trends & Corporate Tenant Profiles
With major IT corridors located within 10 to 15 minutes (Magarpatta, SP Infocity, EON IT Park), Hadapsar Annexe sees sustained rental interest with typical market gross rental yields in the 4.2%–4.8% range.

---

## Conclusion
Buyers seeking immediate walkability to EON Kharadi may favor Kharadi, while those prioritizing master-planned integrated township living, on-campus schooling for children, and larger green footprints find high value at **Kumar Magnacity in Hadapsar Annexe**.
