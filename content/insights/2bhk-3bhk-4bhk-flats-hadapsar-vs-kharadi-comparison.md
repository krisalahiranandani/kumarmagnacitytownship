# 2 BHK, 3 BHK & 4 BHK Flats in Hadapsar vs Kharadi: The 2026 Reality

When buying an apartment in Pune East, prospective homeowners and tech professionals frequently weigh **Hadapsar (Manjari Annexe)** against **Kharadi**. Here is a data-backed comparison of pricing, carpet efficiency, social infrastructure, and capital appreciation.

---

## 1. Price Per Square Foot & Entry Ticket Size

| Parameter | Kharadi Core | Hadapsar Annexe (Kumar Magnacity) | Value Advantage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Average Rate (per sq.ft)** | ₹9,500 – ₹13,000 | ₹6,800 – ₹8,200 | **~30% Higher Value** |
| **2 BHK Price Range** | ₹95 Lakhs – ₹1.25 Cr | ₹72.99 Lakhs – ₹85 Lakhs | **Saves ₹22L+ Capital** |
| **3 BHK Price Range** | ₹1.45 Cr – ₹2.10 Cr | ₹1.05 Cr – ₹1.35 Cr | **Saves ₹40L+ Capital** |
| **Township Scale** | Mostly 2–8 Acre Standalone | **150-Acre Master Township** | **Unmatched Green Cover** |

---

## 2. Infrastructure & Traffic Livability
- **Kharadi Core**: Experiences heavy arterial bottlenecks along Nagar Road and bypass junctions during peak hours.
- **Kumar Magnacity Hadapsar**: Located along the wide Manjari-Solapur corridor with direct signal-free access to Magarpatta, EON IT Park via the new river bridges, and the upcoming PMRDA Ring Road.

---

## 3. Integrated Amenities: Standalone vs 150-Acre Township
While standalone towers in Kharadi offer standard rooftop pools and small gym rooms, Kumar Magnacity provides an expansive **~1 Lakh sq.ft clubhouse**, an on-campus **Podar International School**, Olympic sports courts, and 25+ acres of peripheral oxygen-rich green parks.

---

## 4. Rental Yields & Tenant Demand
Due to lower acquisition costs in Hadapsar Annexe compared to Kharadi, investors in Kumar Magnacity enjoy higher gross rental yields (**4.8% to 5.4% p.a.**) from corporate tenants employed at Magarpatta Cybercity, SP Infocity, and EON Kharadi.

---

## Conclusion
For buyers prioritizing spacious living, on-campus schooling for children, and maximum capital appreciation headroom, **Kumar Magnacity in Hadapsar Annexe** delivers superior long-term ROI compared to saturated pockets of Kharadi.
