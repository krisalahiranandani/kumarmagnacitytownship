# From Hinjewadi to Hadapsar: The East Pune Real Estate Shift

While West Pune (Hinjewadi, Baner, Wakad, Balewadi) has historically been an IT hub, **Pune East (Hadapsar, Magarpatta, Kharadi, Manjari)** has emerged as the clear winner for integrated, master-planned mega-township living.

---

## 1. Why Integrated 150-Acre Townships Outclass Standalone Pockets
In dense western suburbs like Hinjewadi Phase 1-3 or Wakad, land constraints have resulted in fragmented, standalone high-rises with congested access roads and heavy water tanker dependency.

In contrast, **Kumar Magnacity in Hadapsar Annexe** offers:
- Self-contained 150-acre master-planned infrastructure.
- 25+ acres of continuous green lungs and Miyawaki forests.
- In-campus Podar International School eliminating school commutes.
- Direct dual access to Magarpatta Cybercity and Kharadi EON IT Park.

---

## 2. Infrastructure Momentum: Ring Road & Metro Connectivity
The upcoming **PMRDA 128 km Ring Road** and the **Pune Metro Line 3 extension** connecting Hadapsar to the central business districts ensure rapid travel across all Pune zones in under 30 minutes.

---

## Conclusion
For IT professionals seeking expansive living spaces, high green cover, and trusted 60-year developer credentials, **Kumar Magnacity Hadapsar Township** represents the most balanced and future-proof home in Pune.
