## 1. Strategic Location: The Hadapsar-Magarpatta-Kharadi Golden Triangle
Kumar Magnacity enjoys convenient connectivity to Pune East's primary employment epicenters:

- **Kharadi EON Free Zone & World Trade Center (WTC)**: ~15 minutes via the Manjari-Kharadi Bridge corridor.

*(Travel times are approximate driving estimates under normal traffic conditions).*
